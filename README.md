# SaaS-Revenue
SaaS Revenue &amp; Customer Insights Dashboard

Consolidated and analyzed 37,873 sales records to compute revenue, profit, quantity and customer counts. Produced executive KPIs: Total Revenue 2,297.20K, Profit 286.40K, Quantity 38K, Discount 1.56K, Customers 9.99K. Developed an executive Power BI dashboard to track monthly revenue, top products, country/segment performance and customer retention for a SaaS product portfolio. Produced actionable insights that helped prioritize product marketing and retention strategies. Business impact: enabled prioritization of marketing spend to high-performing products and regions and recommended metrics (CLV, Churn, Feature Usage) for deeper user-level tracking.
